# Service logos

`youtube.svg`, `discord.svg`, `lastfm.svg`, `jellyfin.svg` and `navidrome.svg`
are the logos of those services, shown next to the service they stand for.
They are trademarks of their owners; Starlite is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of them.

- YouTube, Discord, Last.fm, Jellyfin: shapes from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org)
  16.37.0 (CC0 1.0), scaled down slightly.
- Navidrome: the line work of the logo in the Navidrome website repository
  (github.com/navidrome/website, `static/brand/navidrome-logo.svg`, Apache-2.0),
  without its colour fills.

Everything else in this folder is from Lucide (see `LICENSE`).
